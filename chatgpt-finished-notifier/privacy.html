Privacy policy

Simple by design.

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Short version: ChatGPT Finished Notifier does not collect, sell, transmit, or analyze your prompts, responses, or browsing history. It runs locally in your browser and only stores your notification preference through Chrome extension storage.

What the extension does

ChatGPT Finished Notifier runs on chatgpt.com to detect when a response starts and finishes. When the relevant ChatGPT tab is hidden, it can show either a custom browser notification or a system notification depending on your selected notification mode.

Data collection

The extension does not collect personal information. It does not collect or store:

  • ChatGPT prompts or responses
  • Conversation history
  • Browsing history outside ChatGPT
  • Names, email addresses, account identifiers, or passwords
  • Analytics, advertising identifiers, or tracking data

Data stored by the extension

The extension stores your selected notification mode, such as Auto, Browser Toast, or System. This preference is stored using Chrome's extension storage API. If Chrome Sync is enabled in your browser, Google may sync that preference between your Chrome installations according to your Chrome account and Google privacy settings.

Network requests and external servers

The extension does not operate its own backend server and does not send your ChatGPT conversation content to any external service. Its notification logic runs locally in Chrome.

Permissions

The extension uses narrowly scoped browser capabilities needed for its features:

  • Notifications: used to show native system notifications.
  • Storage: used to remember your notification-mode preference.
  • Access to chatgpt.com: used to observe the ChatGPT interface state so the extension can determine when a response finishes.

Third parties

ChatGPT Finished Notifier is an independent extension and is not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI. Use of ChatGPT itself remains subject to OpenAI's own terms and privacy practices.

Changes to this policy

If the extension's data practices materially change, this privacy policy will be updated before those changes are published.

Contact and questions

For privacy questions or issues, use the project's public GitHub repository and support page.
